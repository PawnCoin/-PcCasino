What's Broken
1. Dual Admin Authentication (confusing & insecure)
The admin nav button is correctly hidden from non-admins, but the panel itself still has its own separate hardcoded password (pcadmin2024). Two different auth systems doing the same job — one is useless.

2. Wallet Connect is completely fake
The "Connect Wallet" button generates a random hex string and pretends it's a real wallet address. No MetaMask, no WalletConnect, nothing real.

3. Sportsbook bets don't touch your balance
You can click to place a bet on a sports event and nothing happens to your $Pc. Completely disconnected from the wallet system.

4. All data lives only in the browser
User accounts, balance, transaction history, avatar — all stored in localStorage. Clearing the browser = losing everything. No server-side accounts.
